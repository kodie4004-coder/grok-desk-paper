# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Rolling bench. The focus bench rule is unchanged (40+ settled forward paper trades and a 90% cluster-bootstrap high of the after-fee mean below zero, clusters are the Kalshi series or the coin). It is now checked again on every snapshot for each Kalshi, meme and early book that is not a control and not already benched, not only once at registration. A Kalshi book counts only rows after its added day. A book that meets the rule goes into `data/rolling-bench.json` with the time and the numbers that put it there, and it opens no new paper lines from then on. Its weight is 0. Nothing comes off the list on its own, and history stays. The coin-flip control is never benched. On the 2026-11-21 journal the rule adds Momentum vs target (n 319, mean −$0.089, 90% high −$0.026) and Early entry (n 239, mean −$0.195, 90% high −$0.059). Mean revert, wider band is not benched: its 90% high is +$0.001. The Focus card lists rolled books under the collapsed bench with the date and the numbers. The edge gate, graduation, the holdout, the $2 size and the weights of books that are not benched are unchanged. Paper only. Live execution stays off.

- Today header on Home: feed freshness for the Kalshi snapshot and the coin feed (plain-words age, amber after 20 minutes, red after 60), today's after-fee paper P&L split into allocated and shadow books, and a coin repair progress bar (shadow trade count and mean versus the 20-trade positive-mean resume rule). Numbers come from the journals. Paper only. Live execution stays off.
- Focus plan. An offline backtester replays the 805 recorded Kalshi boards in git history through the live entry rules, with the exact taker fee, maker 0, and the journal fill model, and splits in-sample from the last 30% of time. A backtest never graduates a strategy. `scalp-95` (ask above 0.95, 5 to 10 minutes left, $2) is a new challenger pinned at weight 0 until `graduates()` clears on its own forward rows. Books with 40+ forward trades and a 90% upper bound below zero after fees are benched: the five scalp variants, late-tight, late-early, the resting maker, and the three early exit books. They open no new lines; history stays. A Focus card on Home and Score shows only the near-winners, with the bench collapsed. Paper only. Live execution stays off.
- Fee-aware challengers for late favorite and the intra-window scalp. They use the same $2 journal and the same holdout rule. They enter only when the quote mid of the side being bought clears the ask by more than the Kalshi taker fee per contract plus 0.01. That one cent is the fee schedule's rounding unit. It was chosen before any trade from these variants. The quote mid is the proxy because neither bot publishes a win probability. They were added 2026-11-20T23:20:00Z. That day and every earlier day cannot promote or bench them. The 15-minute trainer and the score screen show the break-even win rate for the ask on screen. The edge gate, the loss cap, and the $2 size are unchanged. Paper only. Live execution stays off.
- Phone alerts and a faster early scan. The fast-scan job loops the same early checks about every 60 seconds for 5 hours 50 minutes, commits the board at most every 5 minutes, and a new run starts every 6 hours. The Kalshi chain starts one when none is running. One concurrency group keeps a single run. Jev keeps its 20-call cap and also stops at 100 calls per UTC day. A push goes to ntfy when the hard kills and RugCheck pass and Jev is not a flag. The message says the signal has not passed the edge gate. The topic is a secret and is not shown on the site. Missing secret does nothing. Paper only. Size stays $2. Weight stays 0. Live execution stays off.
- Jev's prompt now includes DexScreener links and description, public X profile facts from syndication.twitter.com when that page parses, a RugCheck summary (mint, freeze, top holders, LP), age, liquidity, and similar tickers from our boards. The choice still carries a confidence. An address cached as unknown before this prompt is asked once more, inside the same 20-call cap. Price and the mint stay out of the request. Weight stays on the existing edge gate. Paper only.
- Early exit shadows use the same $2 entries and a different exit: a 10 minute time stop, a half sale at 12% only when that half clears fees then a 15% trail, and a 15% trail off the peak the 4 second watcher has seen. Those levels were registered before any result. Weight stays 0. The 30 minute time stop now also runs on the watcher, and the snapshot prices an open mint that is not on the safety board, so a flat early position can settle and free its slot. Live execution stays off.
- Early entry is a shadow challenger for Solana coins under 30 minutes old. Hard kills stay on mint, freeze, a known unlocked LP from RugCheck, top 10 over 40%, liquidity under $1,000, and a $2 take-profit that does not clear modeled slippage. A coin already up 3× is a pass. Weight stays 0 unless the existing edge gate clears, and that weight does not allocate. The meme safety bars are unchanged, and a safety sleeve that has already gone to shadow stays there until its own repair gate passes. Discovery rides the Kalshi chain. pump.fun's API still answers with a challenge. Paper only. Size stays $2. Live execution stays off.
- Jev check is a shadow challenger for meme coins. The snapshot asks TypeSafe whether the linked X account is plausibly the real project, whether the coin is a copycat, and whether the description has an obvious rug flag. A flag is compared with the baseline paper book after fees and does not block an entry. Weight stays 0 until the existing edge gate clears. The call is skipped when `TYPESAFE_API_KEY` is missing, cached per contract address, and capped at 20 coins a run. Live execution stays off.
- The meme stop watcher starts from the Kalshi snapshot chain and from the meme snapshot runner, with `gh workflow run`, while a paper coin is open. The 5-minute cron stays a backup. Passes still share one concurrency group. Home health shows when open coins have gone more than 10 minutes without a watch pass. Paper only. Size and the edge gate are unchanged.
- Fast meme stops, a resting Kalshi maker, and a 60-second settlement average. A resting fill requires a later trade strictly below the bid, or an ask strictly below it, because the paper order is last in the queue. A print at the bid is not a fill. The stop checker is a public Actions pass on open paper coins, every 4 seconds, one DexScreener call per pass. The maker and the settlement bot are challengers. They use the existing holdout gate, stay at $2, and do not turn on live execution.
- Repair mode. A loss-cap halt keeps live paper entries off and logs shadow trades. Resume needs 20 shadow trades with a positive after-fee mean, at a fixed $2. The meme sleeve with a non-positive mean over 20 trades is benched the same way. A same-coin re-entry inside an hour of a stop is skipped. A take-profit that does not clear the round trip is blocked. The loss cap, the balance, and the edge gate are unchanged. Paper only.
- The snapshot bet cap no longer counts coin-flip control or shadow logs, so a full book of those lines cannot drop a repair practice trade or an allocated bet. The bet-cap skip is only an allocated pick after 8 bets. Shadow return counts non-skipped practice, not vetoed skips, and a day already cleared is not benched again. The journal role for a shadowed bot is shadow. Size stays $2. Paper only.
