Card 1 of 3

Paper mode

Grok Desk uses fake money on public data. It does not connect a wallet, hold a key, or send an order. The numbers are a paper record. They are not advice and they are not a promise.

Card 2 of 3

What the bots do

The bots record paper bets on 15-minute markets and on coins that pass a fixed safety list. A loss does not make the next bet bigger. A larger paper size needs three separate windows, enough trades, a positive average after fees, and a lower bound above zero. Live execution stays off.

Card 3 of 3

Buying a coin is on you

If you want the coin, you buy it in your own pump.fun or fomo account. Open Coins, copy the contract address with the Copy button, and paste it there yourself. This app cannot do that step. There is no sample address on this card, so you do not paste the wrong mint.
